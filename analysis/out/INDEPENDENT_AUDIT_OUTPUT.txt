[T10b] NOISE-FLOOR GROUPING SENSITIVITY — how much does normalization move it?
  The definition is 'repeated IDENTICAL prompts at temp 0'.  The evaluator
  prompt interpolates the candidate VERBATIM (prompts.EVALUATOR_PREPEND), so
  two candidates differing by whitespace are DIFFERENT prompts and their
  disagreement is not a nondeterminism draw.
  exact bytes (literal identical prompt)      20 / 2790 = 0.0072   (0 of those groups fold >1 distinct raw string)
  NFKC + collapse whitespace (this audit)     20 / 2790 = 0.0072   (0 of those groups fold >1 distinct raw string)
  strip ALL whitespace + strip $ (looser)     24 / 2791 = 0.0086   (6 of those groups fold >1 distinct raw string)
  A grouping that folds >1 raw string is NOT measuring identical prompts:
  it charges genuine format sensitivity to the nondeterminism floor.
