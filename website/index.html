Beyond Division of Labor

How Roles Shape Feedback and Recovery in Multi-Agent Systems

Chih-Hsuan Yang1*, Xinyang Li2*, Rajeev Thakur1, Feng Yan2

1 Argonne National Laboratory2 University of Houston* Equal contribution

Paper Supplement Code Dataset

What do roles change beyond the division of labor?

We hold the agents, topology, call budget and ground-truth evaluator fixed, then delete every role instruction from a Planner–Executor–Reviewer team. Specialization largely disappears, yet first-pass accuracy does not drop. Roles also allocate decision rights: a role-structured Reviewer often withholds approval, so its team submits less and receives less corrective feedback. In exploratory comparisons, the role-free team recovers from failed first answers more often in all five settings, and forcing a submission every cycle closes the gap.
