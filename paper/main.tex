\begin{abstract}
Multi-agent LLM systems are specified as role diagrams --- Planner, Executor,
Reviewer --- and the diagram is read as a mechanism. We ask which phase of the
work that organization carries. Holding agents, call budget, context policy,
verifier and grading fixed, we delete every role instruction (Arm~B) and,
separately, make the division of labor structurally unrepresentable (Arm~C),
across three actor models on $11{,}600$ trajectories from one frozen
snapshot.

Removing roles destroys the division of labor in all three actors: the rate at
which every agent independently asserts its own final answer rises from
$0.000$--$0.120$ to $0.846$--$0.976$, the planner supplying almost all of that
swing. For gpt-oss a logging defect leaves what its planner \emph{computed}
unreadable, so its collapse is established on answer assertion alone. A
rubric-frozen adjudication --- model adjudication, not human annotation,
outcome-blind but \emph{not} condition-blind --- concurs as a second opinion.
First-pass accuracy does not follow: it is unchanged for gemma-4 and improves for gpt-oss
($+0.050$), a parity consistent with prior role ablations and reported here as
our control condition. \textbf{The cost appears only \emph{after} a wrong
first answer --- and, unlike the collapse, not in all three actors.} gemma-4's
recovery falls $0.222 \rightarrow 0.051$ (paired $p=2\times10^{-8}$), and
since its first-pass rates are equal to four decimals that contrast is an
exact reparameterization of the intent-to-treat effect rather than a
post-treatment conditional. gpt-oss falls $0.324 \rightarrow 0.247$ but does
not resolve when paired ($p=0.208$); Llama-3.3 recovers at $0.014$ and $0.015$
--- a genuine null specific to the role manipulation, since enforcement moves
the same actor's recovery to $0.139$. The role-free team is not stopped
early: it holds the same six cycles, spends $5.80$ of $5.83$ on resubmission
rather than revision, and draws \emph{more} oracle verdicts than the
role-labeled team. Architectural enforcement restores organization and
un-freezes answers, restores recovery in Llama-3.3 and final accuracy in
none, at $8$--$17\%$ attrition; our preregistered primary test is null. \textbf{Role structure
\emph{can} be load-bearing for recovery where it is not load-bearing for
first-pass solving} --- shown cleanly in one actor, directionally in a
second, absent in a third, for one topology and an oracle-gated loop.
\end{abstract}
