# AI-assistance disclosure (anonymous, reviewer-facing)

Prepared for the anonymous supplement. **Contains no author, institution,
account, repository or local-path information.** AAMAS asks that where
AI-assisted technologies contribute to hypotheses, methodology or
experimental design, authors disclose the tool, version and role.

This work used them substantially in all three, so the disclosure is
detailed rather than pro forma.

## Tools and versions

| tool | version as retained | role |
|---|---|---|
| Large language model agent (Anthropic Claude, Opus-class) | exact build identifiers not retained for every session | research execution: experiment implementation, campaign operation, analysis scripting, manuscript drafting, internal review |
| Frontier model as a *measurement instrument* | the adjudication model and rubric version are recorded in the released artifact | blinded-by-rubric adjudication of whether a division of labor was realized |

The actor models under study (`gemma-4-31B-it`, `gpt-oss-120b`,
`Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct`) are the experimental subjects, not assistance, and
are reported in the paper.

## What the agents did

- **Hypotheses.** The central hypothesis — that role organization is
  phase-specific, contributing little to first-pass generation and
  materially to post-failure revision — was formed by an agent from
  observed results and then subjected to pre-registered and exploratory
  tests. An earlier hypothesis was falsified by the agents' own analysis
  and retracted; the paper reports that null.
- **Experimental design.** Condition design, budget matching, the
  pre-registration, and the stopping and exclusion rules were drafted by
  agents under human direction and frozen before confirmatory analysis.
- **Execution.** Runner implementation, campaign operation, freezing and
  manifesting.
- **Analysis.** All analysis code. Every manuscript number is generated by
  a canonical script from a hash-manifested frozen snapshot; no numeric
  literal is hand-maintained.
- **Writing.** Manuscript drafting and revision.
- **Internal review.** Several independent agent reviews, including blind
  reviews given only the compiled PDF, and adversarial statistical audits
  tasked with invalidating the paper's own claims.

## What the human author directed and approved

Research direction and every pivot; the decision to pre-register and the
not-submit conditions; the choice of actors and domains; adjudication
replacing human annotation, with the instruction not to overstate what it
establishes; the instruction that the page limit may not be met by hiding
caveats; and final approval of all claims. Scope limits — not overstating
actor generality, not publishing data during review — were human decisions.

## Prompts and records

The exact role prompts, schemas and evaluator prompt **are released in the
artifact**, as experimental material. Session prompts directing the agents'
research work were not retained verbatim; standing instructions are
reconstructed in the project record from the decision log, and we do not
reconstruct prompts that were not retained.

## Completeness, stated plainly

- Session-level prompt transcripts: **not retained**.
- Model build identifiers: **not retained for every session**.
- Experimental prompts, schemas, configs, budgets: **retained and released**.
- Every reported number: **regenerable** from the frozen snapshot by the
  released scripts.
- Decisions, retractions and self-corrections: recorded in the project's
  decision log and claim-status files, including claims the agents
  retracted after finding their own measurement errors.

## Errors found by this process, and disclosed in the paper

Agent review found several defects in the agents' own instrumentation and
analysis, each disclosed in the paper rather than silently repaired: a
logging defect leaving one actor's planner output uninstrumented; a scoring
defect whose direction runs *against* the paper's claim; a recovery
denominator that excluded valid trajectories; and a blinding leak in the
adjudication, which is why the paper does not describe its
condition-contrastive adjudication as blinded.
