\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Role structure is not load-bearing for producing the first answer; it is
load-bearing for recovering from a wrong one. Deleting every role instruction
destroys the functional division of labor in all three actors
($0.846$--$0.976$ of first passes have every agent assert its own answer, the
planner carrying the whole signal, confirmed by a blinded rubric-frozen
adjudication) while first-pass accuracy is unchanged or improves --- a parity
consistent with prior role ablations~\cite{dylan2026equal,%
orogat2026architectural} and reported as our control condition. The cost
appears only after a wrong answer, and not because the role-free team stops
early: it holds the same six-cycle budget, spends $5.80$ of $5.83$ cycles
resubmitting rather than revising, and never revises at all in $0.845$ of
first-pass-wrong trajectories against $0.288$ under roles. What roles
preserve is a second distinct candidate.

This sharpens a known limit rather than discovering one. That models restate
answers under correction is
Degeneration-of-Thought~\cite{liang2024divergent}, persisting under complete
external feedback~\cite{jiang2026exactfeedback,stechly2024selfverification};
the prescribed remedy is multi-agency. Our result inverts that prescription
--- a multi-agent system without role differentiation does not escape it ---
and makes the difference one of degree: the same oracle-gated loop is worth
$+0.044$ to our role-labeled gemma-4 team and $+0.010$ to the identical team
with the labels removed. Arm~C separates what enforcement could have been
doing: making the division of labor structurally unavoidable restores the
organization \emph{and} the recovery, still does not restore final accuracy,
and costs $8$--$17\%$ attrition. Enforcement governs who may \emph{speak},
not whether the agents \emph{differ}, and it is the differing that recovery
needs.

Three guards keep this from overreaching: our pre-registered primary test was
null; the recovery loop is oracle-gated, so none of this is unaided
self-correction; and multi-agent computation is not uniformly valuable in our
own data --- a collapsed gpt-oss team beats its budget-matched single agent
($+0.0294$) while gemma-4's is significantly worse than its own single agent
in both arms ($-0.0225$, $-0.0563$). \textbf{Whether an organization is
load-bearing cannot be read off how much differentiation an intervention
destroys}: it must be measured on the outcome, at the phase where it acts,
with an instrument shown to see the failure it is meant to detect.
