\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Role structure is not load-bearing for producing the first answer; it is
load-bearing for recovering from a wrong one. Deleting every role
instruction destroys the functional division of labor in all three actors
while first-pass accuracy is unchanged or improves --- a parity consistent
with prior role ablations~\cite{dylan2026equal,orogat2026architectural} and
reported as our control condition. The cost appears only after a wrong
answer, and not because the role-free team stops early or gets less
feedback: it holds the same six-cycle budget, draws \emph{more} oracle
verdicts, and never revises its answer at all in $0.845$ of
first-pass-wrong trajectories against $0.288$ under roles. What roles
preserve is the production of a second distinct candidate --- the
prescribed remedy for
Degeneration-of-Thought~\cite{liang2024divergent} inverted. Making the
division of labor structurally unavoidable instead (Arm~C) restores the
organization and un-freezes the answer, and still does not restore
accuracy: enforcement governs who may \emph{speak}, not whether the agents
\emph{differ}, and it is the differing that recovery needs. Three guards
keep this from overreaching: our pre-registered primary test was null; the
recovery loop is oracle-gated; and multi-agent computation is not uniformly
valuable in our own data. \textbf{Whether an organization is load-bearing
cannot be read off how much differentiation an intervention destroys}: it
must be measured at the phase where it acts.
