\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Role structure is not load-bearing for producing the first answer; it is
load-bearing for recovering from a wrong one. Deleting every role
instruction destroys the functional division of labor in all three actors
($0.846$--$0.976$ of first passes have every agent assert its own answer,
the planner carrying the whole signal) while first-pass accuracy is
unchanged or improves --- a parity consistent with prior role
ablations~\cite{dylan2026equal,orogat2026architectural} and reported as our
control condition. The cost appears only after a wrong answer, and not
because the role-free team stops early: it holds the same six-cycle budget,
spends $5.80$ of $5.83$ cycles resubmitting rather than revising, and never
revises its answer at all in $0.845$ of first-pass-wrong trajectories
against $0.288$ under roles. What roles preserve is the production of a
second distinct candidate.

This sharpens a known limit rather than discovering one. That models restate
answers under correction is
Degeneration-of-Thought~\cite{liang2024divergent}; it persists under
complete external
feedback~\cite{jiang2026exactfeedback,stechly2024selfverification}, and the
prescribed remedy is multi-agency. Our result inverts that prescription ---
multi-agency without role differentiation does not escape it --- and makes
the difference one of degree: the same oracle-gated loop is worth $+0.044$ to
our role-labeled gemma-4 team and $+0.010$ to the identical team with the
labels removed. Arm~C separates what enforcement could have been doing:
making the division of labor structurally unavoidable restores the
organization, un-freezes the answer, and still does not restore final
accuracy, at $8$--$17\%$ attrition that leaves its recovery gain resolvable
in only one actor. Enforcement governs who may \emph{speak}, not whether the
agents \emph{differ}, and it is the differing that recovery needs.

Three guards keep this from overreaching: our pre-registered primary test
was null; the recovery loop is oracle-gated, so none of this is unaided
self-correction; and multi-agent computation is not uniformly valuable in our
own data --- a collapsed gpt-oss team beats its budget-matched single agent
($+0.0294$) while gemma-4's team is worse than its own in both arms
($-0.0225$, $-0.0563$). \textbf{Whether an organization is load-bearing
cannot be read off how much visible differentiation an intervention
destroys}: it must be measured on the outcome, at the phase where it acts,
with an instrument shown to see the failure it is for.
