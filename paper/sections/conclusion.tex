\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Multi-agent roles need not improve what a team knows on its first attempt;
their value can appear only after failure, by making external feedback
actionable as revision. Deleting the role instructions destroyed the
division of labor in all three actors while first-pass accuracy was
unchanged or improved, and the difference emerged only once both teams
were told their answer was wrong --- on the same six-cycle budget, drawing
\emph{more} oracle verdicts without roles. One team revised and the other
resubmitted, and in gemma-4 that split became an outcome split
($p=2\times10^{-8}$), again in a second domain. \textbf{Evaluating agent
organization means measuring not just whether a team is correct, but what
its organization lets it do once it learns it is wrong} --- scoped here to
one \PER{} topology, an oracle-gated loop, and a recovery effect clean in
one actor, directional in a second, absent in a third.
