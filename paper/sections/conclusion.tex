\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Multi-agent roles need not improve what a team knows on its
first attempt; their value can appear only after failure, by making
external feedback actionable as revision. Deleting the role instructions
destroyed the division of labor in all three actors while first-pass
accuracy held, and the difference emerged only once both teams were told
their answer was wrong --- on the same budget, drawing \emph{more} oracle
verdicts without roles. One team revised, the other resubmitted, and in
gemma-4 that split became an outcome split (paired $p=2\times10^{-8}$),
again in a second domain. \textbf{Evaluating agent organization means measuring not
just whether a team is correct, but what its organization lets it do once
it learns it is wrong} --- here for one \PER{} topology, an oracle-gated
loop, and a recovery effect clean in one actor, directional in a second,
absent in a third.
