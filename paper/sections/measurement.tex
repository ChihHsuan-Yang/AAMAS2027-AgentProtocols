\subsection{Validation of the measures}
\label{sec:measurement}

The two clause measures were hand-adjudicated against definitions frozen
\emph{before} the adjudication sheet was opened, so they could not be tuned
to flatter the automated scores. Both clear a pre-set threshold of $0.80$:
Planner computation-abstention (\Pone{}) reaches $0.903$, $\kappa=0.806$,
$n=72$; Executor plan-adherence $0.923$, $\kappa=0.843$, $n=13$. \Pone{}
agreement \emph{fell} from $0.967$ at $n=30$ as adjudication continued and
we report the final figure; its residual errors point in \emph{opposite}
directions, so they do not bias the actor comparison.
