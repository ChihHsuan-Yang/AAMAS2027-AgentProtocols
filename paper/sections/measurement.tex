\subsection{Validation of the measures}
\label{sec:measurement}

The two clause measures were hand-adjudicated against definitions frozen
\emph{before} the adjudication sheet was opened, so they could not be tuned to
flatter the automated scores. Both clear a pre-set agreement threshold of
$0.80$: \Pone{} reaches agreement $0.903$ and $\kappa=0.806$ on $n=72$, and
\Etwo{} agreement $0.923$ and $\kappa=0.843$ on $n=13$.

The error structure matters more than those figures. \Pone{} agreement
\emph{fell} from $0.967$ at $n=30$ to $0.903$ at $n=72$ as adjudication
continued, and we report the final figure; its residual errors point in
\emph{opposite} directions, so they do not bias the actor comparison
consistently. One systematic error was repaired and one proposed fix
rejected: a \Pone{} variant adding a symbolic-result detector \textbf{was
rejected when agreement fell to $0.694$}, the detector having fired on 9 of 9
messages judged compliant including the contract's \emph{required}
answer-form section --- penalizing compliance with the contract it was
scoring. Collapse, which carries the headline, is checked separately against a
model adjudication --- outcome-blind but not condition-blind --- and
stress-tested in \S\ref{subsec:collapse}.
