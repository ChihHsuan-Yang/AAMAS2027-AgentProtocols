\subsection{Validation of the measures}
\label{sec:measurement}

The two clause measures were hand-adjudicated against definitions frozen
\emph{before} the adjudication sheet was opened, so they could not be tuned
to flatter the automated scores. Both clear a pre-set agreement threshold of
$0.80$: \Pone{} reaches $0.903$ with $\kappa=0.806$ on $n=72$, \Etwo{}
$0.923$ and $\kappa=0.843$ on $n=13$. \Pone{} agreement \emph{fell} from
$0.967$ at $n=30$ as adjudication continued, and we report the final figure;
its residual errors point in \emph{opposite} directions, so they do not bias
the actor comparison. One proposed fix was rejected: adding a
symbolic-result detector \textbf{dropped agreement to $0.694$}, having fired
on 9 of 9 messages judged compliant including the contract's \emph{required}
answer-form section --- penalizing compliance with the contract it scored.
Collapse, which carries the headline, is checked separately against a model
adjudication --- outcome-blind but not condition-blind --- in
\S\ref{subsec:collapse}.
