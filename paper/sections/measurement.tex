\subsection{Validation of the measures}
\label{sec:measurement}

The two clause measures were hand-adjudicated against definitions frozen
\emph{before} the adjudication sheet was opened, so they could not be tuned
to flatter the automated scores. Both clear a pre-set agreement threshold of
$0.80$: \Pone{} reaches agreement $0.903$ and $\kappa=0.806$ (precision
$0.943$, recall $0.868$, confusion 33/2/3/34) on $n=72$, and \Etwo{}
agreement $0.923$ and $\kappa=0.843$ (precision $0.833$, recall $1.000$) on
$n=13$.

Three properties of the error structure matter more than those figures.
\Pone{} agreement \emph{fell} from $0.967$ at $n=30$ to $0.903$ at $n=72$ as
adjudication continued; we report the final figure and note the direction,
because it is the reason to finish a validation sheet rather than stop at a
good number. The residual errors point in \emph{opposite} directions --- two
false positives are symbolic results carrying no digits, three false
negatives are contract-permitted content --- so they do not bias the actor
comparison consistently. And one systematic error was repaired rather than
tolerated: the first Executor measure matched ``Step 0'' inside the
Executor's own construction, exactly the solving-versus-following confusion
the construct exists to avoid; it was rebuilt to require an explicit plan
reference.

We also built a \Pone{} variant adding a symbolic-result detector to close
the false-positive class. \textbf{It was rejected: agreement fell to $0.694$
and recall to $0.421$}, because the detector fired on 9 of 9 messages judged
compliant, including on the contract's \emph{required} answer-form section.
The ``improvement'' penalized compliance with the contract it was scoring. We
report this because it is the obvious next thing a reader would propose.

\paragraph{Collapse is audited separately.} Because collapse carries the
headline, it is validated against blinded adjudication and stress-tested
against four alternative explanations in
\S\ref{subsec:collapse}: deterministic decoding (ruled out --- no duplicated
group arose from identical prompts), normalization sensitivity (the exact
measure undercounts), retry convergence (ruled out by construction, since the
primary rule reads the first pass), and problem easiness (functional collapse
is high in every difficulty band). That audit is also where we report the
measure's own failure: exact duplication reports $0.000$ for an actor the
adjudication judges collapsed in $0.944$ of sampled trajectories, which is
why the primary measure is functional rather than byte-level.
