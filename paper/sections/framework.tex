\section{Definitions and Measurement}
\label{sec:framework}

\subsection{What ``load-bearing'' means here}

An organization is specified by a tuple: agents, an interaction order, a
message-passing graph, a context-visibility policy, and a \emph{role
contract} per agent. A contract asserts that an agent performs some function
(\textsc{must} decompose the problem) or that it does not (\textsc{must not}
state a final answer); clauses of the second kind make the specification a
\emph{division} of labor. Because a clause is prose handed to a language
model, the \emph{realized} organization is not guaranteed to match and
nothing records which clauses were honored.

\textbf{Definition.} A component of an organization is \textbf{load-bearing}
if removing or altering it reproducibly degrades task performance
\emph{under matched compute and information budgets}. The budget clause
makes it usable: removing role instructions \emph{and} agents, calls or
context tests scale, not organization. Because performance itself
decomposes --- producing a first answer, repairing a wrong one --- the
definition is applied \emph{per phase}, and the two phases answer
differently.

\subsection{Measuring the realized organization}

\paragraph{Functional collapse (primary).} A first pipeline pass
\emph{collapses} if \emph{all three} agents independently assert their own
final answer, making the pipeline three solvers rather than a division of
labor. It targets the property the diagram asserts rather than what a hash
can see, is measured on the first pass so it is not confounded with
trajectory length, and is \textbf{not applicable to the structurally
enforced conditions}, whose schema forbids an answer field.

\paragraph{Exact output duplication (secondary, a lower bound).} Two or more
role messages byte-identical, as \texttt{sha256} of the raw completion before
parsing. Precision $1.000$, recall $0.262$ against the adjudication (a
within-item comparison, so unaffected by that adjudication's condition leak,
\S\ref{subsec:collapse}): it establishes that at least a given amount of
collapse occurred and, its recall differing by actor, is \textbf{never used
to compare how much organization two actors lost}.

\paragraph{Answer authorship.} Whether a Planner authors a final answer ---
the clause structural enforcement makes unrepresentable, and the
manipulation check.

\paragraph{Computation (\Pone{} and density).} Whether a Planner abstains
from computing, scored as containing no evaluated numeric equality
(\textbf{\Pone{}}, a pre-registered fidelity measure). Being the absence of
a pattern it is anti-correlated with length, so every computation claim
cites \textbf{evaluated equations per 1{,}000 characters} instead: the
role-free gpt-oss Planner scores $0.722$ on the binary rate --- apparently
the study's most compliant --- on a 37-character message. A metric that rewards silence
cannot measure a division of labor.

\paragraph{Recovery.} The fraction of trajectories whose first submitted
answer was graded wrong that nonetheless finish correct --- not a new
metric~\cite{jiang2026exactfeedback}; contrasting it across a role ablation
is. It conditions on a post-treatment variable throughout.
