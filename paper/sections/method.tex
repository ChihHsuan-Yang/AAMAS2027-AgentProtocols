\section{Intervention}
\label{sec:method}

To ask whether organization is load-bearing we vary it in both directions from
the same baseline: remove it, and enforce it. All arms share three agents, the
same interaction order (Planner~$\rightarrow$~Executor~$\rightarrow$~Reviewer),
message-passing graph, total call budget, verifier and verifier prompt, retry
budget, decoding parameters (temperature $0$, identical \texttt{max\_tokens}),
stopping rule, and grading. Every trajectory in the frozen snapshot carries
the identical budget record. This is what makes the comparison a test of
organization rather than of scale.

\paragraph{Arm~A --- soft-role \PER{} (control).} Role prompts as ordinarily
written. The organization as specified.

\paragraph{Arm~B --- generic roles (organization removed).} Identical topology
and budget; all three agents receive the same generic prompt (``You are one of
three agents solving this problem collaboratively; continue the work''). Role
names are retained for routing only, so the pipeline still runs and the budget
is untouched. Arm~B is \emph{not} ``\PER{} minus role words'' but the same
topology with no division of labor specified at all.

\paragraph{Arm~C --- architectural enforcement.} Specialization is enforced by
the interface rather than by wording. The \textbf{Planner} emits JSON parsed
against a schema with \emph{no answer field}, so a final answer is
unrepresentable and free text is rejected and regenerated. The
\textbf{Executor} receives the parsed plan object and the problem --- never
the Planner's raw text --- and emits \texttt{\{derivation, answer\}}. The
\textbf{Reviewer} emits \texttt{\{verdict, target\_span, critique\}}, and
\texttt{accept} promotes the Executor's answer verbatim \emph{by reference},
having no field in which to \emph{author} one --- deleting that field outright
would change \emph{who submits} as well as who may \emph{author}. Arm~C
removes no capability: planning, computing, answering and critiquing are all
still performed, each by one agent, under the same call budget. Its cost is
that a rejected emission can exhaust the regeneration budget, the source of
the attrition in \S\ref{subsec:enforce}.

\paragraph{Arm~C$'$ --- partial context control.} Arm~C with shared history
restored in all three role templates; schema, routing and budgets unchanged.
Matching C and A exactly would require feeding raw prose back in, dissolving
the constraint that defines the arm.

\paragraph{Single-agent reference.} A budget-matched single-agent protocol on
the same problems, drawn from a previously released corpus rather than this
campaign (\S\ref{sec:experiments}). Without it, ``collapse costs nothing''
cannot be distinguished from ``the system was one agent all
along''~\cite{zhang2026rethinking}.

\subsection{Instrumentation and pre-registered analyses}
\label{subsec:prereg}

An intervention that never engaged cannot explain an outcome, so engagement is
measured rather than assumed. Every rejected-and-regenerated emission is
logged with its reason; the enforcement rate counts \textbf{role-boundary
violations only}, formatting coercions excluded by a definition fixed in
advance. Answer-bearing calls are counted per arm, so an arm cannot win or
lose by producing fewer answers. One instrumentation gap escaped this
discipline (\S\ref{subsec:logging}).

All endpoints were fixed in writing and committed before any intervention data
existed. Units are (problem, actor) pairs; all arms run on the same problems,
so every test is paired with bootstrap clustering by problem, and P1--P5 carry
a Holm correction. \textbf{P1} intervention $\rightarrow$ fidelity (also the
manipulation check); \textbf{P2} actor $\times$ intervention on fidelity;
\textbf{P3} intervention $\rightarrow$ accuracy; \textbf{P4} actor $\times$
intervention on accuracy, \emph{the primary test of the original thesis};
\textbf{P5} $\Delta$fidelity versus $\Delta$outcome, association only.

Two things belong here rather than in Results, because they concern
provenance. \textbf{First, P4 is null} and we report it as the failure of the
thesis it was written to test. \textbf{Second, the A-versus-B contrast this
paper is organized around was pre-registered as secondary and exploratory},
and is labeled post-hoc everywhere. The pre-registered P1 manipulation
objective --- that enforcement would drive Planner
\emph{computation}-abstention to ${\approx}1.0$ --- also \textbf{failed},
because it named a clause the intervention does not enforce: the schema
forbids emitting an \emph{answer}, and nothing stops a Planner writing
$=1/27$. It was re-designated to answer authorship
(\S\ref{subsec:enforce}).

Exclusions were fixed in advance: \textbf{unparseable trajectories count as
incorrect rather than dropped} --- the rule that decides
\S\ref{subsec:enforce} --- in Arm~C the regenerated message is analyzed and
the rejection count reported, and an arm failing its manipulation check is
reported as having failed it. A not-submit condition was also pre-declared;
\S\ref{subsec:p4null} reports that it was evaluated against the final data and
not met.
