\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\paragraph{The recovery loop is oracle-gated.} The evaluator triggering
retries sees ground truth, and \textbf{no cell recovers before the first
oracle verdict}, so every recovery number is recovery \emph{given} a
ground-truth-informed failure signal --- none of this is unaided
self-correction. Stop-on-correct also censors destructive revision, so this
design cannot measure how often collaboration destroys a correct answer.

\paragraph{Two instrument defects, disclosed rather than repaired.}
First, a \textbf{logging defect}: the runner fell back to a response's
reasoning field only when content was empty, so a short answer carrying a
long hidden reasoning trace was logged as the short answer, affecting
$93.4\%$ of gpt-oss Arm~B first passes. This bounds two things: no point
estimate of that actor's duplication rate is recoverable, \emph{and} its
37-character planner cannot be read as a behavior --- at $2.85$ visible
characters per second against $195$ in its own Arm~A the work was done and
discarded, so we report it uninstrumented, not abstaining. Second, a
\textbf{scoring defect}: the runner records the grade of the last answer
\emph{submitted} while also recording the answer the team last \emph{held},
and these disagree on $17\%$ of Arm~A and $0\%$ of Arm~B first-pass-wrong
trajectories --- a measurement error whose size is
proportional to how much an arm revises --- the behavior our headline is
about. It runs \emph{against} our claim: recoding the 54 trajectories whose
held answer exactly matches gold while recorded wrong (positive control: $0$
of $10{,}734$ non-mismatched rows) makes every recovery gap we report wider.
We keep the pre-registered outcome as primary because changing it after
seeing which way it moves is what preregistration exists to prevent. Neither
defect was fixed mid-campaign, which would have split the corpus under two
outcome definitions.

\paragraph{Every conditional analysis carries a post-treatment label.}
Conditioning on first-pass-wrong, cycles used, accumulated context or
collapse all condition on outcomes of the intervention. The recovery
decomposition is a decomposition, not a causal mediation, and the
unconditional arm contrasts randomized by problem are reported alongside as
the weaker but cleaner statement --- except for gemma-4, whose recovery
contrast is algebraically the intent-to-treat effect
(\S\ref{subsec:recovery}).

\paragraph{Grader noise bounds two of three accuracy contrasts.} Repeated
identical evaluator prompts at temperature $0$ contradict themselves on $20$
of $2{,}790$ distinct graded strings ($0.0072$; $0.0017$--$0.0072$ across
estimators). We do not divide an effect size by that rate --- different
quantities, and the noise largely cancels in a paired contrast --- but
propagate it into the bootstrap: only gemma-4's B$-$A survives
($[-0.0494,-0.0175]$), so gpt-oss's and Llama-3.3's preregistered nulls are
nulls the instrument could not have resolved either way.

\paragraph{The collapse validation is model adjudication, not human
annotation, and not condition-blind.} It used a frontier model against a
rubric frozen before the first judgement, with actor, arm, correctness and
the automated label withheld. It is \textbf{not ground truth}: a single
annotator, no inter-annotator agreement, stability from repeated calls
($0.953$). \textbf{Nor is it condition-blind} (\S\ref{subsec:collapse}): role-token
presence separates Arm~A from Arm~B at $p=1.5\times10^{-24}$ and format
separates Arm~C, so a judge could infer the arm without reading content ---
which is why A1's arm contrast rests on the mechanical measures.
Precision for the byte metric is estimable only where it fires (gpt-oss 14
positives, gemma-4 3, Llama-3.3 none), so we report it per actor and never
pool it. The Arm~C reading is partly mechanical: its validator forbids an
answer field, so some restored ``division of labor'' is the constraint by
construction.

\paragraph{Arm~C's attrition is one-sided and its as-graded gain is
survivorship.} Arm~C leaves $8.0\%$/$9.9\%$/$17.0\%$ of trajectories
ungraded against $0.0\%$/$0.1\%$/$0.7\%$ in Arm~A. Dropping them turns a
significant negative and two nulls into three significant positives ordered
by actor weakness --- the capability-equalizer pattern the preregistration
told us not to submit on. Both scorings are in \S\ref{subsec:enforce} with
the attrition beside them; the pre-registered one is primary. That attrition
is also why Arm~C's recovery gain is resolvable only for Llama-3.3
(\S\ref{subsec:enforce}). Arm~C$'$ is likewise only a \emph{partial}
context control: matching C to A exactly would require feeding raw prose back
in, dissolving the constraint that defines the arm.

\paragraph{Provenance of the headline, and of the single-agent control.} The
pre-registered primary endpoint (P4) is null, and the A-versus-B contrast
this paper is organized around was pre-registered as secondary and
exploratory: an observation meriting pre-registered replication, not a
confirmed result. The \Pone{} manipulation objective also failed and is
reported as such. The single-agent comparator comes from a previously
released corpus, not this campaign, and \textbf{its two actor arms were
produced under different source commits}, making it a directional guard
only.

\paragraph{Three actors, one organization, one domain, one evaluator.} All
three lose the division of labor, but \textbf{Llama-3.3 does not replicate
the recovery result} --- a genuine null specific to the role manipulation
and \textbf{not} a floor (\S\ref{subsec:recovery}). The recovery claim
therefore rests on two actors, one domain and one
Planner--Executor--Reviewer organization. The evaluator is one of the actor
models under study, so its independence from that actor's outputs is not
established.

\paragraph{Measurement blind spots.} Exact duplication undercounts
near-identical outputs and says nothing about semantic redundancy, which we
do not claim to have measured. The functional measure replacing it detects
that every agent asserted its own answer, not that their reasoning was the
same, is inapplicable to Arms~C/C$'$, and has a marker-dependent Arm~A level
(\S\ref{subsec:collapse}). \Pone{} is blind to symbolic results carrying no
digits and is anti-correlated with message length, which is why every
computation claim cites density.
