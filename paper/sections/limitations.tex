\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\paragraph{A logging defect limits what the duplication rate can mean.} Our
runner fell back to a response's reasoning field only when its content field
was empty, so a short answer carrying a long hidden reasoning trace was
logged as the short answer (\S\ref{subsec:logging}). \textbf{This affects
$84.6\%$ of gpt-oss Arm~B messages and $97.4\%$ of its first passes}; the
flag meant to document it reports $5.7\%$.
The campaign was \textbf{not} re-run: the defect was found late, and the
measures the paper's claims rest on --- accuracy, graded from a final answer
that was always present, and functional collapse, read from answer
assertions --- do not depend on the discarded text. We accept in exchange
that no point estimate of gpt-oss's duplication rate is recoverable, that we
cannot claim the hidden computation was redundant, and that our similarity
metrics for that cell describe only the visible tip of what the model
produced. The logger now captures reasoning unconditionally, which does not
repair the data already collected.

\paragraph{The collapse validation is model adjudication, not human
annotation.} The 180-item validation in \S\ref{subsec:collapse} was performed
by a frontier language model against a rubric frozen before the first
judgement, with actor, arm, correctness and the automated label withheld. It
is \textbf{not ground truth}. There was a \textbf{single annotator and
therefore no inter-annotator agreement}; the judge's stability is estimated
from repeated independent calls instead. \textbf{Blinding is partial}: role
labels are retained inside the transcripts, because the question ``did this
agent perform a distinct role function'' is unanswerable without them, so a
determined judge could sometimes infer the arm from content even after we
neutralized the explicit role words. It covers one snapshot of one domain.
And precision is estimable only for gpt-oss, which has 14 flagged positives
in the sample against gemma-4's 3 and Llama-3.3's none, so we report it per
actor and never pool it.

\paragraph{Collapse is post-treatment.} Whether a trajectory collapses is an
outcome of the intervention, not a randomized factor, so conditioning on the
transition (Table~\ref{tab:transition}) yields an association however sharp
its intervals, and licenses no causal reading of
collapse~$\rightarrow$~accuracy. The unconditional arm contrasts, properly
randomized by problem, are reported alongside it as the weaker but cleaner
statement.

\paragraph{Provenance of the headline.} The pre-registered primary endpoint
(P4) is null. The A-versus-B contrast this paper is organized around was
pre-registered as secondary and exploratory, and is labeled post-hoc
throughout. It should be read as a strong, well-audited observation that
merits pre-registered replication, not as a confirmed primary result. The
pre-registered \Pone{} manipulation objective also failed; we report that as
a failed objective before reporting what we learned from it.

\paragraph{Three actors, one organization, one domain.} The result rests on
three actors, on competition mathematics, in one
Planner--Executor--Reviewer organization. All three lose the division of
labor, but only two carry usable accuracy contrasts: Llama-3.3-70B was
launched late, reaches $n=84$ matched problems, and scores $0.22$--$0.35$ on
this benchmark, so we use it to corroborate the collapse rate and draw no
accuracy conclusion from it. With one significant accuracy loss among three
actors we can say that the amount of organization lost does not predict the
cost, which is a negative claim and the one we make; we cannot say what
\emph{does} predict it. The mechanism in \S\ref{subsec:mechanism} proposes
consensus behavior, and testing that properly requires actors selected for
their consensus behavior rather than for their size, which this panel does
not do.

\paragraph{The recovery loop is oracle-gated.} The evaluator that triggers
retries sees ground truth, so ``recovery'' means recovery \emph{with} an
oracle signal --- a property of this protocol family. The mechanism claim is
bounded accordingly: it does not establish that role differentiation aids
recovery under a self-assessed stopping rule. Relatedly, stop-on-correct
censors destructive revision, so this design cannot measure how often
collaboration destroys a correct answer.

\paragraph{Arm~C$'$ is a partial context control.} Arm~C carries less context
than Arm~A by construction, because compact structured objects replace raw
transcripts. C$'$ restores shared history at fixed enforcement and licenses
the contrast ``the effect of adding context at fixed enforcement,'' which is
null. It does \emph{not} make C and A context-matched, and we do not claim
it does; matching them exactly would require feeding raw prose back in, which
would dissolve the constraint that defines the arm.

\paragraph{Attrition is arm-specific.} Arm~C hard-fails on $7.3\%$ and
$5.9\%$ of trajectories against $0.000$ in Arms~A and B, because it rejects
unparseable structured output where the other arms tolerate truncated prose.
We apply the pre-registered ungraded-as-incorrect rule; dropping failures
instead would make Arm~C look better by 6.6 and 8.0 points. Both are
reported. The structural penalty for requiring structured output is
confounded with the intervention and cannot be separated in this design. The
same attrition is why the two grading conventions disagree on C$'-$C for
gemma-4 (\S\ref{subsec:p4null}).

\paragraph{Measurement blind spots.} Exact duplication has precision $1.000$
and recall $0.343$ against blinded adjudication, and normalization raises
the all-message rate from $0.842$ to $0.927$: it undercounts near-identical
outputs and says nothing about semantic redundancy, which we do not claim to
have measured. The functional measure replacing it is also a proxy: it
detects that every agent asserted its own answer, not that their reasoning
was the same, and it is \textbf{inapplicable to Arms~C and C$'$}, where the
schema forbids an answer field, so a zero there would record the constraint
rather than the behavior. \Pone{} is blind to symbolic results carrying no
digits and slightly over-credits compliance; a variant closing this made
agreement substantially worse and was rejected rather than silently adopted.
It is also mechanically anti-correlated with message length, which is why
every computation claim cites the length-robust density instead.

\paragraph{Power and the evaluator.} Interaction endpoints need roughly twice
the sample of a main effect for equal power, so a null on P4 is ``no large
interaction detected.'' The evaluator is one of the actor models under study,
so its independence from that actor's outputs is not established. Backends
have shown large accuracy differences for an identical cell; ours is pinned
and uniform across the run.
