\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\paragraph{The recovery loop is oracle-gated.} The evaluator triggering
retries sees ground truth and \textbf{no cell recovers before the first
oracle verdict}, so every recovery number is recovery \emph{given} a
ground-truth-informed failure signal: not unaided self-correction, and
stop-on-correct censors destructive revision.

\paragraph{A scoring defect that runs against us.} Beyond the
logging defect of \S\ref{subsec:logging}, the runner grades the last answer
\emph{submitted} but records the last answer \emph{held}; these disagree on
$17\%$ of role-structured and $0\%$ of role-free first-pass-wrong
trajectories --- an error proportional to how much a team revises, the
behavior our headline is about. Recoding the $62$ affected trajectories
(positive control: $0$ of $10{,}734$) \emph{widens} every recovery gap; we
keep the pre-registered outcome as primary, since changing it after seeing
which way it moves is what preregistration prevents.

\paragraph{What the conditional analyses are, and are not.}
First-pass-wrong, cycles used, context and collapse are post-treatment
outcomes, so the recovery decomposition is a decomposition and not a causal
mediation --- except for gemma-4, whose contrast is algebraically the
intent-to-treat effect (\S\ref{subsec:recovery}). The other two accuracy
contrasts are nulls the instrument could not resolve, not measured
absences. The collapse validation is \textbf{model adjudication, not human
annotation, not ground truth and not condition-blind}
(\S\ref{subsec:collapse}), which is why the collapse claim rests on the
mechanical measures.

\paragraph{Scope, provenance, and blind spots.} Enforcement's $8$--$17\%$
one-sided attrition (\S\ref{subsec:enforce}) is why its as-graded gain is
survivorship, why its recovery gain resolves only for Llama-3.3, and why
the context-restored condition is only a \emph{partial} control. The
pre-registered primary endpoint (P4) is null and the \Pone{} manipulation
objective failed (\S\ref{subsec:prereg}); the contrast this paper is
organized around was pre-registered as secondary and exploratory --- an
observation meriting replication, not a confirmed result --- and the
single-agent comparator is a directional guard only
(\S\ref{subsec:notsingle}). \textbf{All three actors lose the division of
labor, but the recovery result is carried by gemma-4 alone}: one clean
actor, one \PER{} organization, an evaluator that is itself one of the
actor models under study, and a second domain
(\S\ref{subsec:xdomain}) covering gemma-4 only with a domain-adapted
rather than byte-identical prompt. Finally, the functional measure
detects that every agent asserted an answer, not that their reasoning was
the same.
