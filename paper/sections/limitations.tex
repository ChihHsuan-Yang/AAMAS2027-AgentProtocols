\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\paragraph{The recovery loop is oracle-gated.} The evaluator triggering
retries sees ground truth, so every recovery number is recovery
\emph{given} a ground-truth-informed failure signal: not unaided
self-correction, and stop-on-correct censors destructive revision. An
evaluator that can be queried up to six times favors the team that queries
it most; matched on verdicts, the two teams recover alike
(\S\ref{subsec:mechanism}).

\paragraph{The feedback-matched condition.} It was added after the
snapshot was frozen and is not pre-registered; a re-run of gemma-4's
role-structured condition on $400$ problems shows no endpoint drift, but the
conditions come from separate runs. It matches the role-structured condition on the feedback
string, not on its framing: role-structured agents receive it inside
role-specific instructions, role-free agents as a bare
\texttt{[Evaluator]} entry. On SciBench it was run for gemma-4 and
gpt-oss, the two actors with frozen SciBench cells. The forced-submission
run (\S\ref{subsec:mechanism}) is post-freeze too, covers one actor, one
domain and one run, and removes the whole gate rather than the one clause
that forbids approval after a FAIL.

\paragraph{A scoring defect, which narrows the gap.} Beyond the logging
defect of \S\ref{subsec:logging}, the runner grades the last answer
\emph{submitted} but records the last answer \emph{held}; for gemma-4 these
disagree on $17\%$ of role-structured, $0\%$ of feedback-withheld and $13\%$
of feedback-matched role-free first-pass-wrong trajectories (gpt-oss
$16\%$/$7\%$, Llama-3.3 $6\%$/$0.4\%$, role-structured/feedback-withheld).
Recoding the trajectories whose held answer matched the key but was graded
wrong ($62$ in the nine frozen Omni-MATH cells, positive control $0$ of
$10{,}734$; $3$ in gemma-4's feedback-matched run) moves gemma-4's
role-structured recovery to $0.275$ and the feedback-matched role-free one
to $0.618$: the gap narrows from $0.387$ to $0.343$. We keep the scoring
rule fixed before the data as primary.

\paragraph{Scope, provenance, and blind spots.} Enforcement's $8$--$17\%$
one-sided attrition (\S\ref{subsec:enforce}) is why its as-graded gain is
survivorship and why the context-restored condition is only a
\emph{partial} control. We test one \PER{} organization, with an evaluator
that is itself one of the actors under study; the second domain keeps the
role-free prompt byte-identical but adapts the role-structured and
\emph{evaluator} prompts, so it replicates the mechanism under a matched
protocol, not identical text. The functional measure detects that every
agent asserted an answer, not that their reasoning was the same.
