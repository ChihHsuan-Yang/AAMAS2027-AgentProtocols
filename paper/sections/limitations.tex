\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\paragraph{The recovery loop is oracle-gated.} The evaluator triggering
retries sees ground truth and \textbf{no cell recovers before the first
oracle verdict}, so every recovery number is recovery \emph{given} a
ground-truth-informed failure signal: not unaided self-correction, and
stop-on-correct censors destructive revision.

\paragraph{Two instrument defects, disclosed rather than repaired.} The
\textbf{logging defect} of \S\ref{subsec:logging} bounds two things: no
point estimate of gpt-oss's duplication rate is recoverable, \emph{and} its
37-character planner cannot be read as a behavior, so we report it
uninstrumented rather than abstaining. The \textbf{scoring defect}: the
runner grades the last answer \emph{submitted} but records the last answer
\emph{held}, and these disagree on $17\%$ of Arm~A and $0\%$ of Arm~B
first-pass-wrong trajectories --- an error proportional to how much an arm
revises, the behavior our headline is about. It runs \emph{against} us:
recoding the $62$ trajectories whose held answer matches gold while recorded
wrong (positive control: $0$ of $10{,}734$ non-mismatched rows) widens every
recovery gap. We keep the pre-registered outcome as primary; changing it
after seeing which way it moves is what preregistration prevents.%


\paragraph{Every conditional analysis carries a post-treatment label.}
First-pass-wrong, cycles used, accumulated context and collapse are all
outcomes of the intervention. The recovery decomposition is a decomposition,
not a causal mediation, and unconditional arm contrasts are reported
alongside --- except for gemma-4, whose recovery contrast is algebraically
the intent-to-treat effect (\S\ref{subsec:recovery}). Grader noise bounds
the other two accuracy contrasts: propagated into the bootstrap only
gemma-4's B$-$A survives, so gpt-oss's and Llama-3.3's preregistered nulls
are nulls the instrument could not resolve.

\paragraph{The collapse validation is model adjudication, not human
annotation, and not condition-blind.} It is \textbf{not ground truth}: one
annotator, no inter-annotator agreement, stability from repeated calls
($0.953$); \textbf{nor is it condition-blind} (\S\ref{subsec:collapse}),
which is why the collapse claim's arm contrast rests on the mechanical
measures. Precision for the byte metric is estimable only where it fires
(gpt-oss $14$ positives, gemma-4 $3$, Llama-3.3 none), so we never pool it.
The Arm~C reading is partly mechanical: its validator forbids an answer
field, so some restored ``division of labor'' is the constraint.

\paragraph{Arm~C's attrition is one-sided and its as-graded gain is
survivorship.} Arm~C leaves $8.0\%$/$9.9\%$/$17.0\%$ of trajectories
ungraded against $0.0\%$/$0.1\%$/$0.7\%$ in Arm~A; dropping them turns a
significant negative and two nulls into three significant positives ordered
by actor weakness --- the capability-equalizer pattern the preregistration
told us not to submit on. Both scorings are in \S\ref{subsec:enforce}, the
pre-registered one primary. That attrition is also why Arm~C's recovery gain
is resolvable only for Llama-3.3 and why its first-pass and recovery columns
do not compose into its accuracy row. Arm~C$'$ is only a \emph{partial}
context control.

\paragraph{Provenance, scope, and blind spots.} The pre-registered primary endpoint (P4) is null,
and the A-versus-B contrast this paper is organized around was
pre-registered as secondary and exploratory --- an observation meriting
replication, not a confirmed result. The \Pone{} manipulation objective also
failed, and the single-agent comparator is a directional guard only
(\S\ref{subsec:notsingle}). \textbf{All three actors lose the
division of labor, but the recovery result is carried by gemma-4 alone}:
gpt-oss's paired contrast does not resolve ($p=0.208$) and \textbf{Llama-3.3
does not replicate} --- a genuine null specific to the role manipulation,
\textbf{not} a floor (\S\ref{subsec:recovery}). The claim rests on one clean actor and one
\PER{} organization, with the evaluator itself one of the actor models under
study. It is not confined to one domain: on SciBench ($574$ free-response
college science problems, the complete matched slice) gemma-4's first pass
moves by $0.000$ while paired recovery falls $0.212 \rightarrow 0.020$
($n=99$, $p=6.6\times10^{-5}$), indistinguishable in size from Omni-MATH;
gpt-oss's cell there is underpowered (power
$0.06$--$0.45$ by test) and uninformative. Finally,
the functional measure detects that every agent asserted an answer, not that
their reasoning was the same (Table~1 note).
