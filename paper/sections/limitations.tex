\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\paragraph{The recovery loop is oracle-gated.} The evaluator triggering
retries sees ground truth, and \textbf{no cell recovers before the first
oracle verdict}, so every recovery number is recovery \emph{given} a
ground-truth-informed failure signal --- none of this is unaided
self-correction, and stop-on-correct censors destructive revision.

\paragraph{Two instrument defects, disclosed rather than repaired.} First,
the \textbf{logging defect} of \S\ref{subsec:logging} bounds two things: no
point estimate of gpt-oss's duplication rate is recoverable, \emph{and} its
37-character planner cannot be read as a behavior, so we report it
uninstrumented rather than abstaining. Second, a \textbf{scoring defect}:
the runner grades the last answer \emph{submitted} but records the last
answer \emph{held}, and these disagree on $17\%$ of Arm~A and $0\%$ of
Arm~B first-pass-wrong trajectories --- an error proportional to how much an
arm revises, the behavior our headline is about. It runs \emph{against} us:
recoding the $62$ trajectories whose held answer matches gold while recorded
wrong (positive control: $0$ of $10{,}734$ non-mismatched rows) widens every
recovery gap. We keep the pre-registered outcome as primary; changing it
after seeing which way it moves is what preregistration prevents.

\paragraph{Every conditional analysis carries a post-treatment label.}
Conditioning on first-pass-wrong, cycles used, accumulated context or
collapse all condition on outcomes of the intervention. The recovery
decomposition is a decomposition, not a causal mediation, and the
unconditional arm contrasts are reported alongside --- except for gemma-4,
whose recovery contrast is algebraically the intent-to-treat effect
(\S\ref{subsec:recovery}). Grader noise bounds the other two accuracy
contrasts: propagated into the bootstrap, only gemma-4's B$-$A survives, so
gpt-oss's and Llama-3.3's preregistered nulls are nulls the instrument could
not have resolved.

\paragraph{The collapse validation is model adjudication, not human
annotation, and not condition-blind.} A frontier model against a rubric
frozen before the first judgement, with actor, arm, correctness and the
automated label withheld. It is \textbf{not ground truth}: one annotator,
no inter-annotator agreement, stability from repeated calls ($0.953$). \textbf{Nor is it condition-blind} (\S\ref{subsec:collapse}),
which is why the collapse claim's arm contrast rests on the mechanical
measures. Precision for the byte metric is estimable only where it fires
(gpt-oss $14$ positives, gemma-4 $3$, Llama-3.3 none), so we never pool it.
The Arm~C reading is partly mechanical: its validator forbids an answer
field, so some restored ``division of labor'' is the constraint itself.

\paragraph{Arm~C's attrition is one-sided and its as-graded gain is
survivorship.} Arm~C leaves $8.0\%$/$9.9\%$/$17.0\%$ of trajectories
ungraded against $0.0\%$/$0.1\%$/$0.7\%$ in Arm~A. Dropping them turns a
significant negative and two nulls into three significant positives ordered
by actor weakness --- the capability-equalizer pattern the preregistration
told us not to submit on. Both scorings are in \S\ref{subsec:enforce}; the
pre-registered one is primary, and that attrition is also why Arm~C's
recovery gain is resolvable only for Llama-3.3, and why its first-pass and
recovery columns do not compose into its accuracy row. Arm~C$'$ is only a
\emph{partial} context control.

\paragraph{Provenance, scope, and blind spots.} The pre-registered primary
endpoint (P4) is null, and the A-versus-B contrast this paper is organized
around was pre-registered as secondary and exploratory: an observation
meriting pre-registered replication, not a confirmed result. The \Pone{}
manipulation objective also failed and is reported as such. The
single-agent comparator comes from a previously released corpus, not this
campaign, and \textbf{its two actor arms were produced under different
source commits}, making it a directional guard only. All three actors lose the division of labor, but
\textbf{Llama-3.3 does not replicate the recovery result} --- a genuine null
specific to the role manipulation, \textbf{not} a floor
(\S\ref{subsec:recovery}) --- so the recovery claim
rests on two actors and one \PER{} organization, with the evaluator itself
one of the actor models under study. It is not confined to one domain: on SciBench
($574$ free-response college science problems, the complete matched slice)
gemma-4's first pass moves by $0.000$ while paired recovery falls $0.212
\rightarrow 0.020$ ($n=99$, $p=6.6\times10^{-5}$), a shift indistinguishable
in size from Omni-MATH; gpt-oss's cell there is underpowered ($0.42$) and
resolves nothing. Finally, the functional measure detects that every agent asserted
an answer, not that their reasoning was the same (Table~1 note).
