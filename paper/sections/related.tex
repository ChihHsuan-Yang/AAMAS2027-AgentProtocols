\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\textbf{The question predates the LLM literature that reopened it.}
Organization-centred MAS gave roles a formal existence independent of their
occupants: Moise$^+$ separates a \emph{deontic} specification --- what a
role-filler is obliged and permitted to do --- from the structural and
functional ones~\cite{hubner2002moise}, and OperA makes role adoption an
explicit social contract~\cite{dignum2004opera}. Both treat \emph{when a
role specification binds} as the design problem and answer it with
enforcement outside the agent. An LLM role prompt is the degenerate case: a
deontic clause in prose, with no middleware to make it bind and no record of
whether it did --- a case that tradition would not have called a
specification at all. Our baseline is that case measured; our enforced
condition returns the binding to the interface. Four further literatures
bear on the result.

\paragraph{Role specialization and role drift.} That prompted roles are not
reliably obeyed is established: Cemri et al.~\cite{cemri2025why} name
disobeying the role specification a failure mode from 1{,}600+ annotated
traces; Cao et al.~\cite{cao2026modules} give the training-time account of
\emph{role drift}; Orogat et al.~\cite{orogat2026architectural} find
prompt-level role conditioning does not induce the intended specialization;
Patel~\cite{patel2026representational} measures collapse as near-identical
rationales, Kandoussi~\cite{kandoussi2026whoami} reports differentiation
arising \emph{without} assigned roles, and enforcement-side work makes role
scope structural rather than
advisory~\cite{stettler2026progressive,ge2026roles}. \emph{Owned:} role
collapse, and the decoupling of role quality from task
performance~\cite{cao2026modules,yang2026precise}.

\paragraph{Redundancy, and the parity we do not claim.} Dylan et
al.~\cite{dylan2026equal} hold total LLM calls fixed and find an evolved
Planner--Executor--Critic team no better than an evolved single agent
($p=0.80$). \emph{Owned:} \textbf{first-pass parity under role ablation.} We
report it as a control condition, not a finding, and only a \emph{partial}
replication of their null: gemma-4 and Llama-3.3 are flat, but gpt-oss
\emph{improves} by $+0.0503$ with an interval excluding zero. Zhang et
al.~\cite{zhang2026rethinking} argue reported multi-agent gains are setup
artifacts of structural collapse --- an objection aimed at our role-free
condition, which is why \S\ref{subsec:notsingle} runs that control and
reports
that it cuts \emph{both} ways; Chen et al.~\cite{chen2026agentslimming}
prune redundant agents on the same premise.

\paragraph{Failure to revise, and answer lock-in.} That models restate
answers under external correction is established for complete verifier
feedback~\cite{jiang2026exactfeedback} and binary
backprompts~\cite{stechly2024selfverification}; the phenomenon has a name,
\textbf{Degeneration-of-Thought}~\cite{liang2024divergent}, and belief
entrenchment is studied directly~\cite{oh2026entrenchment}. Huang et
al.~\cite{huang2024selfcorrect} are frequently misread here: their
\emph{oracle} condition, which uses the correct label as a stopping rule,
\textbf{improves} accuracy ($75.9 \to 84.3$ on GSM8K) and only the
\emph{intrinsic} condition degrades. Our loop is oracle-gated like theirs.
Closest to our lever is Chen et al.~\cite{chen2026relabeling}, who hold an
erroneous claim byte-identical and vary only the \emph{role label} it
carries, moving explicit-correction rate $23$--$93$pp --- the strongest
published evidence that a role label is causal for error correction, but
\textbf{single-agent}, with chat-template message roles rather than agent
personas, and no recovery conditional or budget matching. \emph{Owned:} the
failure, the name, and recovery-after-error as a
metric~\cite{jiang2026exactfeedback,liang2024divergent}. Not owned is the
\textbf{inversion}: multi-agency is the prescribed cure for
Degeneration-of-Thought, and a multi-agent system without role
differentiation does not escape it.

\paragraph{Premature consensus, which we test rather than propose.} Early
convergence is a widely proposed explanation for multi-agent failure and
sustaining diversity a widely proposed remedy~\cite{oh2026entrenchment};
self-correction's limits are surveyed by~\cite{kamoi2024when}. \emph{We
propose neither.} \S\ref{subsec:mechanism} treats premature stopping as a
\textbf{standing hypothesis and rejects it} on matched realized budget. Our
lever is the role \emph{label}, not a diversity intervention --- and one
actor's content diversity runs opposite to its recovery, which is why we do
not call diversity the active ingredient.

\paragraph{Coordination versus capability.}
Canedo~\cite{canedo2026architecture} frames structured specification as a
\emph{capability equalizer} whose value falls with model strength;
\cite{yu2026bottleneck} reaches the same prediction from theory and
Dochkina~\cite{dochkina2026drop} the complementary case. Our enforced
condition reproduces their monotone ordering exactly --- \emph{but only under a
scoring rule that drops $8$--$17\%$ of trajectories}, and under our
pre-registered rule it disappears (\S\ref{subsec:enforce}). We report both
because a reader who re-derives either needs to find the other.

\paragraph{What is open, stated narrowly.} These literatures establish four
things --- roles are often not obeyed; deleting them leaves first-pass
accuracy intact; models fail to revise under external feedback; structure
tends to help weaker models --- each about a \emph{different stage of the
work}, and no study holds them in one frame. So the field has an implicit
synthesis, that role prompts are decorative, which no single experiment has
tested. \textbf{It has not been tested because the two findings most
responsible for it were measured in different places}: parity at the first
answer, failure-to-revise after feedback and almost always single-agent,
where there is no organization to ablate. Nothing licenses carrying parity
across that phase boundary, and nothing has stopped the field from doing
so.

\paragraph{Relation to the corpus this work instruments.} Three prior
studies analyze
it~\cite{jiang2026confidence,yang2026protocol,yang2026precise} and our
single-agent control is drawn from its released form; two are
observational, the third varies how critique \emph{reaches} the
solver~\cite{yang2026precise}. \textbf{None manipulates the role structure
itself}, none measures whether the specified division of labor is realized,
and the controlled conditions of \S\ref{sec:method} do not exist in that
corpus.
