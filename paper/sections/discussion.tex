\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{What a role diagram is evidence of.} Both interventions changed the
realized organization dramatically and final accuracy barely at all, in
opposite directions. A diagram is therefore a hypothesis about a system, and
task-outcome evaluation --- the only evaluation most multi-agent systems
receive --- cannot test it. The phase decomposition is what makes ``the roles
are working'' and ``the system is accurate'' separable: an aggregate score
averages over a stage where the organization is idle and a stage where it is
doing all of its work.

\paragraph{Why the monotone expectation fails.} Capability-dependence
accounts predict one ordering: structure helps weaker models and matters less
as models improve~\cite{canedo2026architecture,yu2026bottleneck}, with the
self-organization literature drawing the same line from the other
end~\cite{dochkina2026drop}. Our panel does not order that way, and the
mechanism says why: what roles supply is not scaffolding for a weak solver
but a second distinct candidate after a failure. Our weakest actor needs it
least, being too weak for the recovery channel to exist at all.

\paragraph{The confound we tested rather than argued.} A reader can object
that the role-free team recovers less because its context is dirtier --- it
accumulates $5.8$ failed submissions against $1.5$ --- and that context
contamination is an independently established degradation
path~\cite{yang2026contamination}. The effect is real and large within every
arm, and it does not account for the arm gap at matched context in the lower
quartiles. But in gpt-oss's top quartile the ordering \emph{reverses}: where
context is longest, the role-labeled arm's own contamination costs it more
than the roles return. The mechanism claim is therefore bounded, not clean.

\paragraph{What our own measurement errors imply.} Two instruments in this
study reported confidently on things they could not see. An
exact-duplication metric returned $0.000$ for actors a blinded judge found
collapsed, because their agents paraphrase rather than copy; a logging defect
reduced one actor's planner to $37$ visible characters, which we nearly
reported as a behavior before its $2.85$ characters per second showed the
work had been done and discarded. Any claim that a multi-agent system's
structure is intact should state what the instrument would have to see to
report otherwise, and show that it can.
