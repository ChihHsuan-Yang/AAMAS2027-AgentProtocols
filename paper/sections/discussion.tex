\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{What a role diagram is evidence of.} Both interventions changed the
realized organization dramatically and final accuracy barely at all, in
opposite directions. A diagram is therefore a hypothesis about a system, and
task-outcome evaluation --- the only evaluation most multi-agent systems
receive --- cannot test it. The phase decomposition is what separates ``the
roles are working'' from ``the system is accurate'': an aggregate score
averages a stage where the organization is idle with one where it does all of
its work.

\paragraph{Why the monotone expectation fails.} Capability-dependence accounts
predict one ordering: structure helps weaker models and matters less as models
improve~\cite{canedo2026architecture,yu2026bottleneck,dochkina2026drop}. Our
panel does not order that way, and the mechanism says why: what roles supply
is not scaffolding for a weak solver but a second distinct candidate after a
failure. Our weakest actor needs it least, being too weak for the recovery
channel to exist at all. Testing this properly needs actors selected by
consensus behavior rather than size.

\paragraph{The confound we tested rather than argued.} A reader can object
that the role-free team recovers less because its context is dirtier --- it
accumulates $5.8$ failed submissions against $1.5$ --- and contamination is an
established degradation path~\cite{yang2026contamination}. The effect is real
and large within every arm, and does not account for the arm gap at matched
context in the lower quartiles. But in gpt-oss's top quartile the ordering
\emph{reverses}: where context is longest, the role-labeled arm's own
contamination costs more than the roles return. The mechanism claim is
bounded, not clean.

\paragraph{What our own measurement errors imply.} Two instruments here
reported confidently on what they could not see. An exact-duplication metric
returned $0.000$ for actors a blinded judge found collapsed, because their
agents paraphrase rather than copy. A logging defect reduced one actor's
planner to $37$ visible characters, which we nearly reported as a behavior
before its $2.85$ characters per second showed the work had been done and
discarded. Both modes are general: any claim that a multi-agent system's
structure is intact should state what the instrument would have to see to
report otherwise, and show that it can.
