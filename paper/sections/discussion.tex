\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{What a role diagram is evidence of.} Both interventions changed
the realized organization dramatically and the score barely at all, in
opposite directions. A diagram is therefore a hypothesis about a system, and
task-outcome evaluation --- the only evaluation most multi-agent systems
receive --- cannot test it. This is not an argument against role
specifications but against conflating two claims: ``the roles are working''
and ``the system is accurate'' require separate measurements. The corollary
for interface design is that enforcement governs speech, not thought.

\paragraph{Why the monotone expectation fails.} Capability-dependence
accounts predict one ordering: structure helps weaker models and matters less
as models improve~\cite{canedo2026architecture,yu2026bottleneck}, with the
self-organization literature drawing the same line from the other
end~\cite{dochkina2026drop}. Our panel does not order that way, and the
mechanism says why the monotone framing cannot accommodate it: what roles
supply is not scaffolding for a weak solver but \emph{sustained
disagreement}, and whether an actor needs that supplied externally is a
property of its consensus behavior, not its capability. This predicts where
the result should generalize --- any actor reaching near-total agreement with
itself should depend on differentiation for recovery regardless of capability
--- and testing it requires actors selected by consensus behavior rather than
size, which this panel does not do. It also blocks the efficiency reading: a
pruning decision validated on one substrate does not transfer, and what to
measure instead is whether \emph{your} actor escapes its own wrong
consensus.

\paragraph{What our own measurement error implies.} Our first reading, that
the actors lost different amounts of organization, did not survive a blinded
judge: the exact-duplication metric cannot see an actor whose agents
paraphrase rather than copy. The failure mode is general, so any claim that a
multi-agent system's structure is intact should state what the instrument
would have to see to report otherwise, and show that it can.
