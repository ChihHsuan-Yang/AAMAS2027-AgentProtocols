\subsection{Campaign}
\label{sec:experiments}

\paragraph{Task and problems.} Competition mathematics (Omni-MATH), sampled
by a deterministic seeded manifest so every prefix of the run is itself a
valid random sample. All arms run on the same problems, so every arm
comparison is paired at the problem level. A second domain was designed and
dropped: its Planner contract carries no computation clause, so its
components would not be comparable.

\paragraph{Actors.} Three actor models served from one vLLM endpoint:
\textbf{gpt-oss-120b}, \textbf{gemma-4-31B}, and \textbf{Llama-3.3-70B}. The
third was added late and reaches $n=125$ matched problems, enough to
corroborate the collapse rate and not enough for an accuracy contrast; we use
it only for the former. Two further candidates
were probed and rejected as unavailable rather than assumed available. The
evaluator is gpt-oss-120b in every cell, which is one of the actors under
study --- see \S\ref{sec:limitations}.

\paragraph{Scale.} 9{,}079 trajectories over 59{,}989 agent messages:
${\approx}1{,}315$ problems per arm for Arms~A, B, and C in each of two
actors, plus 400 per actor in Arm~C$'$ and ${\approx}128$ per arm for the
third actor. Paired accuracy contrasts rest on
1{,}310--1{,}319 matched problems per actor. Concurrency was set per actor at
levels measured on the live endpoint (25 for gpt-oss, 10 for the gemma
family) rather than assumed; gemma-4 is four to five times slower per call,
so arms do not complete in equal wall-clock at equal concurrency.

\paragraph{Power.} Computed by simulation (4{,}000 replicates, two-sided
$\alpha=0.05$) from discordance rates in the pre-existing corpus. The paired
per-actor main effect is well powered for large effects at this scale, while
the actor~$\times$~intervention interaction needs roughly twice the sample:
\textbf{a null on P4 therefore means ``no large interaction detected,'' not
``no interaction.''} Organizational measures are computed per message, so the
measurement half of the study is well powered at trajectory counts at which
the outcome half is not.

\paragraph{Gates and a confound caught before it became a result.} The main
run was gated on a smoke test requiring non-zero Arm~C boundary rejections
with reasons logged, answer-bearing calls equal across arms, and a pinned
backend. An early campaign was stopped and quarantined after 121 trajectories
when $74\%$ of Arm~C rejections turned out to be the model cut off at the
token limit mid-JSON rather than violating a role boundary. Truncation was
not arm-specific ($8.4\%$/$11.3\%$/$8.9\%$ over A/B/C) but its \emph{cost}
was: Arms~A and B tolerate a truncated prose message where Arm~C rejects
unparseable JSON. Left alone this would have produced a convincing
``enforcement lowers accuracy'' result that was a token-budget artifact. The
token limit was doubled, a logged salvage path added, and the pre-fix rows
quarantined and never pooled.
